## 01 Business Diagnostic (`#diagnostic`)

A fixed-fee look inside your kitchen and your books: what everything really costs, where the money and the hours go, and the fixes that matter most.

[Fixed fee $___]

What is included:
- Review of your books, sales mix and last months of costs
- Recipe and plate costing on your top sellers
- Labor, scheduling and prep walk-through
- A written report with your real costs and a ranked list of fixes

What you have at the end: A clear picture of where the business stands and what to fix first, whether or not you keep working with Rob.

## What every engagement covers (cream band)

**Eyebrow:** INSIDE EVERY OFFER

**Heading:** What every engagement covers.

- **Setup** — Books, chart of accounts, budget and the first-year plan. Formation, permits and licensing go to a Texas attorney or CPA.
- **Costing and accounting** — Recipe and plate costing, COGS, pricing, P&L structure and a monthly close you can keep.
- **Operations and SOPs** — Prep systems, ordering, inventory, staffing, HR and written standards the crew actually follows.
- **Production** — Batch scaling, co-packing, time studies and the move from kitchen to shelf.
