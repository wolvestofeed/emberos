## 5. What we stand for

**Heading:** Built on four commitments

**Complete transparency**
Every figure shows where it came from: sourced, stated, derived or placeholder. A placeholder never looks like a real number.

**Regenerative and sustainable**
Energy, water, waste, refrigerants and supplier footprint are measured in the same system that runs production, not in a spreadsheet off to the side.

**Nutrition first**
Food is judged by what it does for the body. Cotyledon scores every flat against a client's nutrition targets, and each benefit cites its research source.

**Hospitality and community**
Food is how people gather. Customer, client and family portals bring the people being fed into the system, with their orders, invoices and choices in one place.

## 6. Engines and modules

**Heading:** A complete operating system, not a slice of one

**Sub:** Most food software covers one piece: inventory, or point of sale, or a CRM. Ember OS runs the whole operation from one data model.

[Grid of ten tiles. Each tile: name + one line. Link under the grid: **See every module →** /platform]

1. **Production engine** — Plans, batches or sowings, schedules, capacity and time studies, from one plan of record.
2. **CPA-grade accounting** — Double-entry ledger, US GAAP perpetual inventory at actual cost, P&L, balance sheet and cash flow.
3. **Unit economics** — True cost per unit and cost to serve, built from tagged inputs.
4. **Sustainability** — Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions, energy, water, waste, refrigerants and supplier life-cycle data.
5. **Supply chain** — Procurement, purchase orders and a supplier directory with what each supplies.
6. **Inventory and safety** — FIFO lots and full traceability; HACCP control points or the Produce Safety Rule.
7. **People** — Staffing, schedules against labor demand, SOPs and training.
8. **Sales and clients** — Sales portal, prospects, customers and subscribers, orders.
9. **Portals** — Customer, client, supplier and family portals, each in its own space.
10. **Billing and payments** — Invoicing, receivables and payables, and subscriptions billed through Stripe.

## 7. Transparency spotlight

**Heading:** Every number shows its work
